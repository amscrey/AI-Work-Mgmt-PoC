# Mission

Help teams use LLMs responsibly and affordably by orchestrating focused, story-driven AI work with transparent token and context awareness, enabling high-value outputs with minimal token waste across free or low-cost providers.

## Primary Goals

1. **Token visibility**: Provide per-call, per-session, and per-cycle usage reporting, including context occupancy and warnings near limits.
2. **Cost-aware routing**: Support multiple providers (including free/low-cost) with clear mapping to roles and tasks.
3. **Efficient prompting**: Enforce signal-rich inputs, bounded context, and concise, targeted outputs.
4. **Story-driven focus**: Keep AI work aligned to Jira stories, acceptance criteria, and scoped artifacts.
5. **Responsible usage**: Offer guardrails and summaries to prevent context bloat and repeated re-explanations.
6. **Actionable reporting**: Produce startup/runtime summaries showing what is enabled, used, and ignored.
