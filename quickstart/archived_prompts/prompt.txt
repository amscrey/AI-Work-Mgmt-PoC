- **Game rules overview**
  - What parts of the original Jaws board game should remain?

Let'ss focus only on Act1 from the original game.  We'll remove the cards.  We'll condense the Hooper and Quint into a single "Boat" role.

We'll leave the board (with modified space setup), the barrels, swimmers, and other playing pieces.

- **Player setup**
  - Will the game support exactly 2 players, or both 2 and 3 players?
Both 2 and 3.  One player plays the shark - mandatory.  In a two player game the 2nd player plays both Brody and the Boat - in a 3 player game each is played separately.

Some initial ideas:

Objective
---------
Shark:
Eat enough swimmers to win.
Humans:
Save/clear swimmers and use detection/barrels to limit the shark.
Prevent the shark from reaching its victim target before the round limit.


Board markers / game pieces
---------------------------
Swimmer markers
Barrel markers
Brody pawn
Boat pawn
Shark pawn (only used when spotted)
Radar marker
Binoculars marker

Board
-----
Shore spaces Each of these has land + adjacent sea presence:
East Beach
North Beach
South Beach
West Beach
Amity Police Dept. (barrel depot)
Mayor’s Office
West Docks (barrel pickup)
East Docks (barrel pickup)

Open water spaces:
Open Water North
Open Water West
Open Water South
Open Water East

Setup / Initial placement
-------------------------
Brody starts at Amity Police Dept.
Boat starts at West Docks or East Docks
Shark starts secretly in any open water space

Turn structure
--------------
Each round:
  Brody takes 3 actions
  Boat takes 3 actions
  Shark takes 3 actions
  Resolve end-of-round swimmer placement / victory check

Barrels
-------
Barrels begin in supply at: Amity Police Dept.
Can be staged at: West Docks, East Docks
